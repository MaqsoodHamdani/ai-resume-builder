# Candor

Candor is a career workspace for job seekers: analyze a resume, fix what the report finds, build a new one from templates, and tailor it to a specific job description.

## Users
- Students and recent graduates writing a first resume.
- Mid-level and senior professionals refreshing a resume for a move.
- Career changers translating experience into a new field.

They open Candor in focused sessions of 20 to 60 minutes, mostly on a laptop, sometimes checking a report on a phone.

## Product truths
- Feedback is specific and derived from the user's own text. Never generic.
- Honest: the score is an internal indicator, not a guarantee of passing an employer's ATS. Never suggest adding skills or experience the user does not have.
- AI suggestions never overwrite content silently: original, suggestion, accept, reject.
- No invented testimonials, customer logos, partnerships or business statistics.

## Voice
Plain, direct, encouraging without hype. Labels name actions ("Analyze resume", "Download PDF"). Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it.

## Brand
- Name: Candor. Mark: a page corner folded into a "C".
- Light theme. Ground #FFFFFF / #F8FAFC, ink #111827, muted #64748B, line #E5E7EB, accent #2563EB.
- Signature: the annotated page. The resume itself with findings pinned in its margin.

## Surfaces
- Marketing site (/, /features, /pricing): Persuade.
- Auth and onboarding: Operate.
- App workspace (/dashboard/*): Operate.

## Anti-references
Generic AI dashboards: gradient heroes, glass panels, KPI tiles with icons in tinted circles, emoji, purple/indigo, eyebrow labels, rows of identical icon cards.
